# Activity-first discovery — 23 October 2026

The main route now starts with an activity, a relevant follow-up and practical date/format/price choices. No persona or cultural seed is required. Exact optional Qloo seeds retain favourite/curious labels. Prior tools and browser saves remain available at `/classic`.

`/api/opportunities` gathers existing official calendar adapters, available personal Fever results, the dated official-release catalog and category-based Qloo places concurrently. Adapters produce one Opportunity contract: source URL, kind, dates, location, evidence, checked time, price or explicit unknown, and availability caveats. Existing bounded source readers retain timeouts, byte limits and ten-minute request coalescing. Partial provider failure leaves successful records usable. This is an on-demand pipeline, not a scheduled crawler.

Shared eligibility applies date overlap, source freshness, closures, requested format, activity, explicit price handling and dismissed IDs. URLs are canonicalized to remove tracking duplicates while retaining separate dated occurrences. Ranking uses explicit activity/direction evidence and confirmed cultural mentions or Qloo contributors; favourites count more than curiosity. Provider affinity only breaks place ties. Source diversification reduces domination by a busy calendar. No cross-provider confidence percentage is invented.

Live checks found and fixed substring false positives (Collective/AZATOY), music merchandise classified as live entertainment, and shops selling games classified as places to play. All have regression coverage. Other tests check opposite follow-ups changing rank, price unknowns, expiry, closure dates, rejected IDs, canonical deduplication, impossible dates and provider failure isolation in Workers.

Observed seed-free live inventory: 136 official records (five source families), 25 Qloo collecting records before deduplication and 8 current catalog entries. Different answers change eligibility and order; this does not establish human satisfaction. Local Fever requires the hosted identity and is not counted as a successful authenticated live test.

Known limits: five official source families remain a small coverage sample; this release adds no X crawler or new external credentials. Source wording can remain Japanese. Genre/style evidence is often absent from music calendar titles, so these are labelled activity-only matches. No crowd inference, semantic personality model, venue-programme verification, stock guarantee or exhaustive Tokyo inventory. Saved items are a shortlist, not automatic preference inference. Dismissal reasons explain hiding one item; they do not silently generalize to a whole category. Legacy and new browser saves are kept separately and both remain reachable.
