# SideQuest demo guide

Aim for roughly two minutes. Confirm the competition's actual video and presentation rules before recording.

## Before the demo

Open the private site while signed in as its owner. Confirm Qloo responds and the catalog has current records. Use the browser's own saves only if you intend to show them. Do not expose environment files, bearer tokens or OAuth callbacks while recording.

Routes depend on Tokyo time. The example below was checked on 25 October 2026. If recording at another time, rehearse first and describe the result that actually appears. A screenshot is a recorded result, not a live fallback.

## Talk and actions

**0:00–0:20.** “I know what music I like. I don't necessarily know which Tokyo doors to open. SideQuest starts with one thing I love and turns it into a short local quest.” Enter Nujabes and choose Records & listening.

**0:20–0:45.** While the four stages run, explain that Qloo resolves the artist and supplies cultural associations. SideQuest ranks a reviewed venue pool, checks opening windows, compares short loops and reads venue pages. State that these are actual operations, not a timed loading animation.

**0:45–1:10.** Show the resulting stops and one authored mission. Open The connection to show the distinction between Qloo evidence and the chosen activity. Open the map and show that the return walk is included. Avoid presenting weekly hours as a guarantee of today's access.

**1:10–1:35.** Change the direction to Art & photography. Show what actually changes. The checked example brings in Gallery Conceal and removes the music-only extra leads. Explain that the answer constrains recommendations rather than generating a personality label.

**1:35–1:50.** Open Events & drops, then Vinyl & record fairs. Point to BIG LOVE or pianola and its official source link. A shop listing date is not proof of a limited release or current stock.

**1:50–2:00.** “The next test is whether people choose to go. We've fixed specific bad matches and measured coverage; we haven't demonstrated a human-rated quality uplift yet.” End on the quest, not on settings.
