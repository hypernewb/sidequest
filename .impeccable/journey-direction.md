# Catalog, map, journey and visits

Operate mode. Extend the existing MomentFeed surface. The user wants many choices, geographic planning and one enjoyment rating after a visit. They chose a single rating without follow-up reasons. They supplied a Google Maps browser key after initially choosing the fallback map.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Browse current opportunities, put chosen finds into a day, then teach the recommender through actual enjoyment. The catalog remains the first task; an empty planner must not displace it.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit SideQuest paper, serif headings, red actions, moss selection and ruled lists from DESIGN.md. No new visual identity or generated imagery.

STORY: Browse the range, open Map & plan, select up to five stops, set a date and optional arrival times, reorder, open Google Maps. After attending or trying something, give one 1-to-5 rating. Visit history allows edits and forgetting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Existing title and filters lead. All finds, Map & plan and Been & rated are adjacent controls before results. Empty planning controls only open with Map & plan. A nonempty day stays visible above results. On mobile, planner controls stack with reachable move/remove actions.

FORM: Narrow extension of an established surface. No concept seed required. Group current listings at shared venue coordinates; show missing-location listings and online finds in the catalog. Google Maps gets a checked coordinate or a specific venue query, never an invented point.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Verification includes Google map success, selection, Google route URL order, rating persistence and ranking changes, desktop and mobile. No quality-lift claim or automatic attendance tracking. Browser-local history only.
